Partners & Sponsors

We're grateful to the organizations that make our mission possible. Their support helps us bring free engineering education to students.

Boss Orthodontics

Boss Orthodontics in Bee Cave has been a generous supporter of our mission, vision, and goals. Granting us a donation toward our project fund, they have helped us expand our method of instruction and give more opportunity to our students.

Visit Website →

iCode Bee Cave

iCode is a premier coding and STEM education school empowering kids and teens with real-world tech skills. Their Bee Cave location has been a tremendous supporter of our mission to grow the next generation of engineers.

Visit Website →

Kreators Lab

Kreators Lab is dedicated to inspiring young innovators through creative coding and technology education. Their partnership helps us expand our reach and bring even more hands-on learning to our students.

Visit Website →

Lake Travis Community Library

The Lake Travis Community Library has generously provided us with space to run our workshops, making our programs accessible to the local community. Libraries like this are the backbone of community learning.

Visit Website →

SAFE Foundation

UpDraft Engineering’s partnership with SAFE Foundation brings hands-on STEM learning to more students around the world. By combining SAFE Foundation's work expanding access to robotics and technology in underserved schools with UpDraft's student-led engineering education, we're helping more young learners explore, build, and discover what's possible through engineering.

Visit Website →
